📖 WHAT THIS BUILDS ON
Biodiversity: Three Levels
Reading 34.01 and Lesson 34.01
Before you begin: finish Reading 34.01 and work through Lesson 34.01, including the three levels of biodiversity (genetic, species, ecosystem) and why greater diversity brings stability and resilience.
Lesson focus: Variety at three scales, and why it keeps a living world standing
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts: match each example to its level of biodiversity, answer one true/false about diversity and stability, and explain in a sentence or two why a more diverse ecosystem is more resilient. It should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What does variety — measured at three scales — actually do for a living world?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Match example to level (5 pts, 1 each). Match each example to the correct level of biodiversity from the list below.
Example Level (write the letter)
Many alleles for disease resistance within a wild rabbit population 
Fifty balanced fish species on one reef vs. five on another 
Florida's mix of springs, swamps, scrub, mangroves, and reef 
The Florida panther's dangerously low genetic variation 
The number and evenness of tree species in a forest 
A. Genetic diversity (variety within a species)  ·  B. Species diversity (number and evenness of species in a community)  ·  C. Ecosystem diversity (variety of habitats across a region)
2
Part B — True or false (2 pts). Write True or False and correct it in a few words if false: “An ecosystem with more species is generally less stable, because there are more things that can go wrong.”
3
Part C — Explain the resilience (3 pts). In one or two sentences, explain why a food web with many species is more resilient to the loss of one species than a simple web is. Use the idea of redundancy.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A: rabbit alleles → A, reef fish species counts → B, Florida habitat mix → C, panther variation → A, tree-species number/evenness → B.
☐
Part B: False — greater diversity generally makes an ecosystem MORE stable and resilient, because more species means redundancy (backups) in the food web.
☐
Part C explains that in a diverse web several species fill similar roles, so if one is lost others take up its part and energy keeps flowing; a simple web has no backup and collapses.
