Before you begin: finish Reading 31.02 and work through Lesson 31.02, including the 10% rule, the energy pyramid, and the key distinction that energy flows (and is lost as heat) while matter cycles.
Lesson focus: Only ~10% of energy passes up; the rest is lost as heat
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts: do the 10% arithmetic for an energy pyramid, correct a common misconception about energy, and explain why food chains are short. It should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If only a tenth of the energy makes it up each step, what does that do to the shape of an ecosystem — and where does all the missing energy go?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Do the 10% math (5 pts, 1 each). The producers store 50,000 energy units (Level 1). Apply the 10% rule and fill in each value.

3
Part C — Why chains are short (3 pts). In two or three sentences, use the 10% rule to explain why food chains rarely have more than four or five links and why top predators are few.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A: Level 2 = 5,000; Level 3 = 500; Level 4 = 50; about 0.1% of Level 1's energy remains at Level 4.
☐
Part B: False — energy flows one way and is ultimately lost as heat; it is not recycled. Only matter (atoms) cycles through an ecosystem.
☐
Part C explains that ~90% of energy is lost at each step, so after a few levels too little remains to support another — keeping chains short and top predators few.
