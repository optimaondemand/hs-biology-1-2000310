Lesson focus: why the evidence supports endosymbiotic theory
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has two parts. In Part A you match each line of evidence to what it shows. In Part B you write a short structured response — claim, evidence, reasoning — arguing why two specific clues support endosymbiotic theory. This is your in-lesson CER practice made gradable; the larger module CER essay is a separate, later assignment. The whole assignment should take about 20–25 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
How can four small clues inside an organelle convince scientists that it was once a free-living bacterium?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Match the evidence to what it shows (5 pts). For each line of evidence in the left column, write the letter of the matching explanation from the right column. Each is used once.
Line of evidence What it shows
1. Own circular DNAA. The organelle reproduces by splitting in two, the way bacteria do.
2. Own ribosomesB. Genetic material like a bacterium’s, separate from the nucleus.
3. Double membraneC. The organelle makes its own proteins on bacterium-like machinery.
4. Divides by binary fissionD. Expected if a host membrane wrapped around an already-membraned cell.
2
Part B — A short CER (5 pts). In four to six sentences, argue whether the evidence supports the claim that mitochondria and chloroplasts were once free-living prokaryotes. Your answer must include: (a) a Claim (does the evidence support the theory?); (b) Evidence — name and describe the circular DNA and the double membrane; and (c) Reasoning — explain why each of those two clues is what you would expect if the organelle had once been an independent bacterium.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
All four lines of evidence are correctly matched to what they show.
☐
Part B states a clear claim and describes both the circular DNA and the double membrane as evidence.
☐
The reasoning explains why each clue fits an ancient free-living cell — not just that it is “evidence.”
📊 GRADING RUBRIC
Criteria Proficient Developing Beginning Pts
Evidence matching
5 pts		 All four lines of evidence correctly matched. Two or three correctly matched. One or none correctly matched. 5 pts
CER reasoning
5 pts		 Clear claim; both clues described; reasoning explains why each fits a free-living origin. Claim and evidence present, but reasoning is thin or covers only one clue. No clear claim, or evidence is listed without reasoning. 5 pts
Total: 10 pts
