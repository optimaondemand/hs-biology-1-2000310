📖 WHAT THIS BUILDS ON
Cell Theory: Two Centuries of Seeing
Reading 5.01 and Lesson 5.01
Before you begin: finish Reading 5.01 and work through Lesson 5.01, including the three tenets and the four scientists who built cell theory.
Lesson focus: how we came to know that all living things are made of cells
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has two parts. In Part A you match each scientist to the contribution they made to cell theory. In Part B you explain, in your own words, why cell theory counts as a well-supported scientific theory rather than a guess. The whole assignment should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Cell theory took two hundred years to build. What made each step possible — and why do we trust the finished theory today?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Match the scientist to the contribution (5 pts). For each scientist in the left column, write the letter of the matching contribution from the right column. Each is used once.
Scientist Contribution
1. Robert Hooke (1665)A. First to observe living microorganisms (“animalcules”).
2. Antonie van Leeuwenhoek (1670s)B. Established that all cells come from pre-existing cells.
3. Schleiden & Schwann (1838–39)C. Coined the word “cell” after viewing cork.
4. Rudolf Virchow (1855)D. Concluded that all plants and all animals are made of cells.
2
Part B — Why a theory, not a guess (5 pts). In three to five sentences, (a) state the three tenets of cell theory in your own words, and (b) explain why scientists call it a theory — that is, what kind of support stands behind it. Use the idea that cell theory was built from many independent observations over time, and that no exception has ever been found.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
All four scientists are correctly matched to their contributions.
☐
Part B states all three tenets accurately in the student’s own words.
☐
Part B explains why cell theory is well-supported (many independent observations, no exceptions found).
