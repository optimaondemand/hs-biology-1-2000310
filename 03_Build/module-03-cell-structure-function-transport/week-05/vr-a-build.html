Week 5 · VR-A (Build / Model)
Build a Prokaryote and a Eukaryote Side by Side
Solo VR activity · ~30 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🦠 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will build two cells next to each other in space — a simple prokaryote and a more complex eukaryote — and label what makes them different. Reading that “prokaryotes have no nucleus” is one thing; placing (or deliberately leaving out) a nucleus with your own hands makes the contrast stick. This is the comparison from Lesson 5.02, built so you can walk around it.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or a desktop VR app · your completed Lessons 5.01–5.02 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Cell Modeling room (Optima VR library)
On arrival: go to the workbench marked by the Whiteboard IFX. A sticky note there gives your first instruction.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Build the prokaryote. Start with a cell membrane shell, add a cell wall around it, and place the DNA loose inside as a nucleoid — with no nucleus and no membrane-bound organelles. Keep it small.
2
Build the eukaryote beside it. Make it larger, with a cell membrane and a true nucleus wrapping the DNA. Add at least two membrane-bound organelles (for example a mitochondrion and, if you wish, a chloroplast for a plant cell). Add a cell wall only if you are modeling a plant cell.
3
Label the key differences. Place sticky notes marking: the nucleus (present in the eukaryote, absent in the prokaryote), the membrane-bound organelles, the nucleoid, and the cell membrane on each cell. Add one note pointing to whichever cells have a cell wall, reminding yourself that the wall is not the membrane.
4
Record your walkthrough. Start a spatial recording. Walk between your two cells and explain aloud, in about 60–90 seconds: the single defining difference (the nucleus), one other difference, and why every cell still has a membrane even though only some have a wall.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your spatial recording to this Canvas assignment. If you can’t record, submit one snapshot of your two labeled cells plus a few typed sentences naming the defining difference (the nucleus) and explaining wall versus membrane.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 30 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students externalize the prokaryote/eukaryote contrast as two spatial models. Common sticking points: deliberately leaving the nucleus out of the prokaryote (students often add one by habit), and remembering that a cell wall is separate from the membrane. Watch for students who treat “wall” and “membrane” as the same thing — nudge them to label the membrane on both cells and the wall only where it belongs. A strong submission shows both cells, the nucleus present in one and absent in the other, at least two organelles in the eukaryote, and a clear spoken or written account of the differences.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
Both cells are built: a small prokaryote (nucleoid, no nucleus) and a larger eukaryote (true nucleus, organelles).
☐
Sticky notes label the nucleus, organelles, nucleoid, membrane, and wall (where present).
☐
A spatial recording (or snapshot + written explanation) is submitted that explains the differences.
