Week 6 · VR-B (Explore / Observe)
Explore Inside a Cell — Find Each Organelle by Its Function
Solo VR activity · ~20 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔎 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will walk through the inside of a giant cell and play detective: at each station you are given a clue about an organelle’s function, and you must find and identify the organelle it describes. Reading a list of organelles is one thing; recognizing the Golgi from “I package and ship the cell’s products” while standing next to it makes function the thing you remember, not just the name.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Lessons 6.01–6.04 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Inside the Cell — Organelle Walk (Optima VR library)
On arrival: a Web URL Image IFX by the entry shows your function-clue checklist. Begin there.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Follow the function clues. Walk through the cell. At each station you’ll read a clue such as “I store the DNA and control the cell,” “I release energy as ATP,” or “I package and ship the cell’s proteins.” Find the organelle each clue describes and place a sticky note on it with its name.
2
Solve the “who packages proteins?” clue carefully. One clue reads “I package and ship the proteins after they are built and folded.” Be sure your sticky note lands on the Golgi apparatus — not the ribosome (which builds) and not the ER (which folds). On your note, add one phrase saying what the ribosome and the ER do instead.
3
Capture your evidence. Take four named snapshots, each showing one organelle you identified and its clue — be sure one is the Golgi and one is the mitochondrion. Note whether this cell has a cell wall and chloroplasts (is it a plant or animal cell?).
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your four named snapshots to this Canvas assignment, plus a short typed note: name each organelle you found and its function, state clearly which organelle packages and ships proteins, and say whether the cell you explored was a plant or an animal cell and how you could tell.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 20 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students explore a cell-interior Location, match function clues to organelles, annotate with sticky notes, and capture named snapshots. Common sticking points: placing and typing on sticky notes, and taking a named snapshot rather than a system screenshot. The key learning check is the Golgi clue — watch for students who tag the ribosome or ER instead. Encourage them to reason from the clue’s verbs (“package and ship” = Golgi). A complete submission identifies at least four organelles by function, names the Golgi correctly as packager/shipper, and tells plant from animal by the presence or absence of a cell wall and chloroplasts.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
At least four organelles are found from their function clues and annotated by name.
☐
The Golgi is correctly identified as the organelle that packages and ships proteins, distinct from the ribosome and ER.
☐
Four named snapshots and the typed note (including plant vs. animal) are submitted to Canvas.
