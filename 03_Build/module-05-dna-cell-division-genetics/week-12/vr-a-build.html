Week 12 · VR-A (Build / Model)
Model Transcription → Translation at a Ribosome
Solo VR activity · ~30 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔬 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will build this week’s pathway with your hands. First, transcribe: from a DNA template strand, snap together a matching mRNA strand — remembering to use U in place of T. Then carry that mRNA to a ribosome and translate: bring tRNAs whose anticodons match each codon, and watch a short amino-acid chain assemble. A flat diagram hides the two-step, two-place logic of the central dogma; building it in three dimensions makes it real. This is Lessons 12.01 and 12.02, built in space.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Lessons 12.01 and 12.02 · a codon chart · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Molecular Modeling room (Optima VR library)
On arrival: go to the workbench marked by the Whiteboard IFX. A sticky note there gives your first instruction.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Read the template. Pick up the DNA template strand at the bench. Note its bases — this is what RNA polymerase will read.
2
Build the mRNA (transcription). Snap a complementary mRNA base onto each DNA base (A→U, T→A, G→C, C→G). Double-check: did you use U wherever the DNA had A? No thymine allowed in RNA.
3
Carry mRNA to the ribosome & find the start. Move your mRNA to the ribosome model. Identify the start codon (AUG) and place a marker on it — this is where reading begins.
4
Translate. For each codon, bring the tRNA whose anticodon matches, and snap its amino acid onto the growing chain. Continue codon by codon until you reach a stop codon.
5
Record your walkthrough. Start a spatial recording. Walk your build and explain aloud, in about 60–90 seconds: where transcription happened vs. translation, the start codon you found, and how anticodons matched codons. Use the words transcription, codon, and anticodon.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your spatial recording to this Canvas assignment. If you can’t record, submit labeled snapshots of your built mRNA and your amino-acid chain plus a few typed sentences identifying the start codon and explaining how each tRNA anticodon matched its mRNA codon.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 30 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students externalize the central dogma — transcribing a DNA template into mRNA (U for T), then translating it at a ribosome via matching tRNA anticodons. Common sticking points: writing T instead of U in the mRNA, and confusing codon (on mRNA) with anticodon (on tRNA). Nudge students who skip the start codon or forget to stop. A strong submission shows a correctly transcribed mRNA, a correctly assembled amino-acid chain begun at AUG, and a recording that uses “transcription,” “codon,” and “anticodon” correctly.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
A complementary mRNA strand is built from the DNA template, with U used in place of T.
☐
The start codon (AUG) is identified and a short amino-acid chain is assembled by matching tRNA anticodons to codons, ending at a stop codon.
☐
A spatial recording (or labeled snapshots + written explanation) is submitted.
