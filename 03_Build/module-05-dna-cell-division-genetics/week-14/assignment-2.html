Lesson focus: Cut + combine + paste = recombinant DNA; gel electrophoresis separates fragments by SIZE
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts: put the recombinant-DNA steps in order, answer two questions about gel electrophoresis, and read a simple gel result. The whole assignment should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
How do scientists cut a gene out, paste it into a carrier, and then read the fragments they have made?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Order the steps (4 pts). Number these recombinant-DNA steps 1 to 4 in the correct order: ( ) DNA ligase seals the gene into the plasmid · ( ) A restriction enzyme cuts the gene and the plasmid, leaving sticky ends · ( ) The recombinant plasmid is taken up by bacteria, which make the protein · ( ) The matching sticky ends of the gene and plasmid pair up.
2
Part B — Gel electrophoresis (4 pts, 2 each). Answer both: (i) Does gel electrophoresis separate DNA fragments by their charge or by their size? (ii) Do smaller or larger fragments travel farther from the wells?
3
Part C — Read the gel (2 pts). On a gel, Band 1 sits close to the wells and Band 2 sits far from the wells. Which band is the larger DNA fragment, and how do you know? Answer in one sentence.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A order: cut (1) → sticky ends pair (2) → ligase seals (3) → bacteria take up and express (4).
☐
Part B: by size (not charge); smaller fragments travel farther.
☐
Part C: Band 1 (near the wells) is larger, because larger fragments travel less far.
