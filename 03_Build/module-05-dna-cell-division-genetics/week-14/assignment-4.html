📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has two parts: a short labeling task on how CRISPR works, and a brief written response taking a reasoned position on a germline-editing question. The second part is teacher-reviewed. The whole assignment should take about 20–25 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
We can now rewrite the code of life. The harder question is not whether we can — but whether, and where, we should.
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — How CRISPR works (4 pts, 1 each). Match each item to its role by writing the correct letter.

2
Part B — Take a position (6 pts). In 5–8 sentences, choose one question about germline editing — consent for a future person, the line between curing and enhancing, or fairness and access — and argue a clear position. State your view, give at least one reason supporting it, and name one honest objection someone might raise against you. (There is no “correct” position; you are graded on the quality of your reasoning.)
