Lesson focus: PCR amplifies a target DNA region into billions of copies through repeated cycles of three steps
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts: fill in a table of the three PCR steps, explain the link back to natural replication, and name one real use of PCR. The whole assignment should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
How does a machine that only changes temperature turn one piece of DNA into billions of copies?
