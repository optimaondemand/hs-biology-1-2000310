Lesson focus: some inheritance patterns go beyond a simple dominant/recessive pair
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Three parts: classify two patterns as incomplete dominance or codominance, match ABO genotypes to blood types, and solve one X-linked color-blindness cross. The whole assignment should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why does a recessive trait carried on the X chromosome show up far more often in sons than in daughters?

Part C — X-linked cross (4 pts). A carrier mother (XCXc) has children with a father of normal vision (XCY), where c = color blindness. Draw the Punnett square (2 pts), then state what fraction of the sons are expected to be color blind and explain why (2 pts).
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A: pink snapdragons = incomplete dominance; AB markers = codominance.
☐

☐
Part C square is correct and concludes 1/2 of the sons are color blind, because sons get their single X from the carrier mother.
