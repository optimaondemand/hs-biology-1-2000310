Week 13 · VR-A (Build / Model)
Build Dihybrid Punnett Squares in 3D
Solo VR activity · ~30 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔬 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will build a 4×4 dihybrid Punnett square with your hands. Place a heterozygous parent’s four gametes (RY, Ry, rY, ry) along the top, the other parent’s four down the side, then combine them to fill all 16 boxes. Finally you will sort the offspring by appearance and tally the famous 9:3:3:1 ratio. On paper a sixteen-box grid is easy to mis-copy; building it in space forces you to track every allele. This is Lesson 13.02, built by hand.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Lesson 13.02 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Genetics Lab — Punnett Workbench (Optima VR library)
On arrival: go to the large gridded workbench marked by the Whiteboard IFX. A sticky note there gives your cross and your first instruction.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Read your cross. The sticky note sets the cross RrYy × RrYy (R = round dominant to r = wrinkled; Y = yellow dominant to y = green). Build an empty 4×4 grid from the frame pieces in the parts bin.
2
Place the gametes. Snap the four gamete tiles RY, Ry, rY, ry along the top axis, and the same four down the left axis. These are the only gametes a double-heterozygote can make.
3
Fill all 16 boxes. For each box, combine the gamete from its row with the gamete from its column to build the offspring genotype (for example RY × rY → RrYY). Drop the correct allele tiles into every box.
4
Sort and tally. Color-flag each box by its phenotype (round-yellow, round-green, wrinkled-yellow, wrinkled-green) and count each group. You should land on 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.
5
Record your walkthrough. Start a spatial recording (or take a labeled snapshot). In about 60–90 seconds, walk your grid and say which gametes you used, how you filled one box, and the 9:3:3:1 ratio you found.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your spatial recording to this Canvas assignment. If you can’t record, submit one labeled snapshot of your completed 4×4 grid plus a typed line stating the 9:3:3:1 ratio you tallied.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 30 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students externalize a dihybrid cross by placing the four gametes on each axis, filling all sixteen boxes, and sorting by phenotype to recover 9:3:3:1. Common sticking points: writing only two gamete types instead of four (forgetting independent assortment), and miscounting the phenotype groups. Nudge students who produce fewer than four gamete tiles per parent. A strong submission shows a correctly filled 4×4 grid, a phenotype tally of 9:3:3:1, and a recording (or snapshot + note) that reports the ratio.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
A 4×4 grid is built with the four gametes (RY, Ry, rY, ry) on each axis.
☐
All 16 boxes are filled and sorted by phenotype into a 9:3:3:1 tally.
☐
A spatial recording (or labeled snapshot + the ratio found) is submitted to Canvas.
