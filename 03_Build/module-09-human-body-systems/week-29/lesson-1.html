📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Microbes are on your skin, in the air you breathe, on everything you touch. So why are you not sick right now?

Two lines, one style

Innate immunity is the defense you are born with. Its style is captured in three words: fast (minutes to hours), general (it treats most invaders alike), and no memory (it responds the same way every time). It works in two lines. The first line is barriers — your skin (the integumentary system from Lesson 26.03), mucus and cilia in your airways, and stomach acid — that try to keep invaders out entirely. The second line is the inner response — inflammation, fever, phagocytes, natural killer cells, and the complement system — that attacks whatever gets through.

💪 THE IDEA THIS BUILDS

Your first defenders are always on duty: barriers to keep invaders out, and a fast, general inner response to attack whatever gets in — no training, no memory, no delay. It is the body's quiet self-government at its most automatic. In the next lesson you meet the defenders that make up for the one thing this system lacks: the ability to learn a specific enemy and remember it.
