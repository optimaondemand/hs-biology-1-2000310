Lesson 10.03
Mirror Processes: Photosynthesis ↔ Respiration
Lesson · Includes in-lesson CER practice
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Photosynthesis builds glucose; respiration spends it. So is respiration just photosynthesis run backward?
It is the most tempting idea in this module — and it does not quite hold. Today you will build a careful comparison of the two processes, see why they are complementary but not exact reverses, correct a stubborn misconception (plants respire too!), and practice arguing the question in a short in-lesson CER.

🪞 PART 1 · THE APPARENT MIRROR

Two equations that look like opposites

Written as summary equations, the two processes really do appear to mirror each other:

Photosynthesis: CO₂ + H₂O + light → glucose + O₂
Cellular respiration: glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + ATP

What one consumes, the other produces. That balance is no accident — it is the very thing that ties every breathing creature to every green leaf. But an equation hides the machinery, and that is where “reverse” falls apart.

⚖️ PART 2 · COMPLEMENTARY, NOT REVERSE

If you could film both processes, you would not see one running the other in rewind. They use different organelles, different enzymes, and different intermediate steps in a different order. The Calvin cycle is not the Krebs cycle backward. They reach mirror-image endpoints by entirely separate roads — so they are best called complementary: fitted to each other, each supplying what the other needs, without being the same reaction.

⚠️ A MISCONCEPTION TO FIX · DO PLANTS RESPIRE?
Many people think plants only photosynthesize and animals only respire. Not so. Photosynthesis only stores energy in glucose — to actually use it (grow roots, open flowers, move materials) a plant cell must break that glucose back down in cellular respiration, exactly as your cells do. So plant cells do both: in the light they photosynthesize and respire; in the dark, with no light for photosynthesis, they keep right on respiring. A plant is not the opposite of an animal — it is a producer that also lives off its own product.

💪 THE IDEA THIS BUILDS

Complementary, not identical, not opposite — that fit is the key that unlocks the rest of biology. The oxygen you breathe was released by a leaf storing energy; the carbon dioxide you exhale will feed the next leaf. That ceaseless exchange is this module’s thread of service, and it is exactly the story you will follow one atom at a time in the final lesson.
