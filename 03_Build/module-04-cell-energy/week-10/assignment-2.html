Counting the ATP: Aerobic vs. Anaerobic

Before you begin: finish Reading 10.02 and work through Lesson 10.02, including the ATP tally by stage and the role of oxygen.
Lesson focus: tally ATP by stage and compare aerobic (~36–38) with anaerobic (2)
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has two parts. In Part A you complete an ATP-tally table by stage and total it. In Part B you compare the aerobic and anaerobic totals and explain, in one sentence, the role of oxygen. Use “about” values, just as in the lesson. About 15 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Same molecule of glucose, two very different harvests. Why does a cell with oxygen get nearly twenty times as much ATP?
