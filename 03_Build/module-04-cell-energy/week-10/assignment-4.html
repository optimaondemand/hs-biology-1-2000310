📖 WHAT THIS BUILDS ON
The Journey of a Carbon Atom
Reading 10.04 and Lesson 10.04
Before you begin: finish Reading 10.04 and work through Lesson 10.04, including the carbon-atom journey and the rule that matter cycles while energy flows.
Lesson focus: trace a carbon atom and distinguish one-way energy flow from cycling matter
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In Part A you trace a labeled carbon atom through a short scenario, naming the process at each arrow. In Part B you write two sentences distinguishing energy flow from matter cycling. About 15 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If a carbon atom can travel air → leaf → animal → air over and over, why can’t energy do the same?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Name the process at each arrow (6 pts, 2 each). Follow carbon atom “C” through the scenario below. For each arrow, name the process that moves the carbon, and say which organelle (if any) is responsible.
Arrow in the journey of carbon atom “C” Process (and organelle)
CO₂ in the air → glucose in a leaf 
Glucose in the leaf → glucose in an animal that eats the leaf 
Glucose in the animal → CO₂ back in the air 
Hint: the middle arrow is not photosynthesis or respiration — it is simply eating (the carbon is passed along when the animal consumes the plant).
2
Part B — Energy vs. matter (4 pts). In two sentences, explain the difference between how energy and matter move through this journey. Make clear that the carbon atom cycles (reused again and again) while energy flows one way (entering as sunlight, leaving gradually as heat).
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Arrow 1 = photosynthesis (Calvin cycle, chloroplast); arrow 2 = the animal eating the plant; arrow 3 = cellular respiration (mitochondrion).
☐
Part B states that the carbon atom cycles (is reused).
☐
Part B states that energy flows one way (sunlight in, heat out) and is not recycled.
