Week 9 · VR-A (Build / Model)
Build a Mitochondrion & Map the Three Stages
Solo VR activity · ~30 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔬 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will build a mitochondrion with your hands — the outer membrane, the inner membrane folded into cristae, the matrix inside, and the narrow intermembrane space — then place labels showing where each stage of respiration happens. A flat diagram hides the layers; building it in three dimensions makes the addresses obvious, and makes clear the one that surprises students: glycolysis happens outside the mitochondrion, in the cytoplasm. This is Lesson 9.01, built in space.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Lesson 9.01 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Molecular Modeling room (Optima VR library)
On arrival: go to the workbench marked by the Whiteboard IFX. A sticky note there gives your first instruction.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Build the two membranes. Place a smooth outer membrane around the whole organelle. Inside it, build the inner membrane and fold it into deep ridges — the cristae. Notice how the folding packs in surface area.
2
Name the two spaces. Label the fluid-filled center enclosed by the inner membrane as the matrix, and the narrow gap between the two membranes as the intermembrane space.
3
Place the three stages. Put a sticky note for the Krebs cycle in the matrix, and one for the electron transport chain on the inner membrane. Then — this is the key move — place the glycolysis label outside the mitochondrion entirely, in the surrounding cytoplasm, with a note that it needs no oxygen.
4
Record your walkthrough. Start a spatial recording. Walk your model and explain aloud, in about 60–90 seconds: name each part, and say which stage happens where — making sure to point out that glycolysis is in the cytoplasm, not the mitochondrion.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your spatial recording to this Canvas assignment. If you can’t record, submit one labeled snapshot of your mitochondrion plus a few typed sentences naming each part and the stage that happens there.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 30 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students externalize the mitochondrion’s structure and map the three respiration stages onto it. The single most important check: the glycolysis label must sit in the cytoplasm, outside the organelle — this is the most common student error. Watch also for the inner membrane being drawn smooth rather than folded into cristae, and for the matrix and intermembrane space being swapped. A strong submission shows a correctly layered mitochondrion, all four structures labeled, the three stages placed in the right locations (glycolysis outside), and a recording that names them aloud.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
A mitochondrion is built with outer membrane, inner membrane folded into cristae, matrix, and intermembrane space all labeled.
☐
Krebs cycle is placed in the matrix, the ETC on the inner membrane, and glycolysis OUTSIDE in the cytoplasm.
☐
A spatial recording (or labeled snapshot + written explanation) is submitted that names the parts and stages.
