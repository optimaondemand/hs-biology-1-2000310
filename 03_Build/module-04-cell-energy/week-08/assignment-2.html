Before you begin: finish Reading 8.02 and work through Lesson 8.02, including the photosynthesis equation, the parts of the chloroplast, and the two stages and where they happen.
Lesson focus: structure explains function — the chloroplast and the equation
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three parts: label the chloroplast’s parts and their jobs, write the photosynthesis equation and identify reactants and products, and place the two stages in their locations. The whole assignment should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Where, exactly, inside a green cell does air and sunlight become sugar — and why does the chloroplast’s structure suit the job?

3
Part C — Place the stages (2 pts). In two to three sentences, state which stage of photosynthesis happens in the thylakoid membrane and which happens in the stroma.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
All four chloroplast parts are correctly described (thylakoid = light reactions; stroma = Calvin cycle).
☐
The equation is written correctly with reactants (CO₂, H₂O, light) and products (glucose, O₂) identified.
☐
The light reactions are placed in the thylakoid membrane and the Calvin cycle in the stroma.
