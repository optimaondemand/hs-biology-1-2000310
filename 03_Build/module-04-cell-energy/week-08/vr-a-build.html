Week 8 · VR-A (Build / Model)
Build a Chloroplast & Trace the Light Reactions
Solo VR activity · ~30 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔬 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will build a chloroplast with your hands — the double membrane, the thylakoids stacked into grana, and the fluid stroma around them. Then you will mark where each stage of photosynthesis happens and trace the path of energy along a thylakoid: light → excited electrons → ATP + NADPH. A flat diagram hides how the two stages sit in two different places; building it in three dimensions makes the geography obvious. This is Lessons 8.02 and 8.03, built in space.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or a desktop VR app · your completed Lessons 8.02 and 8.03 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Molecular Modeling room (Optima VR library)
On arrival: go to the workbench marked by the Whiteboard IFX. A sticky note there gives your first instruction.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Build the outer shell. From the parts bin, place the chloroplast’s double membrane — two membranes wrapping the whole organelle. This boundary is a clue to its endosymbiotic past, from Module 3.
2
Stack the thylakoids. Place flattened thylakoid sacs and stack several into a pile to make a granum. Build two or three grana inside the shell. The fluid you leave around them is the stroma.
3
Mark the two stages. Place a sticky note on the thylakoid membrane reading “light reactions here” and a sticky note in the stroma reading “Calvin cycle here.” Add a label on the double membrane.
4
Trace the energy path. Along one thylakoid, lay down arrows or markers showing the flow: light hits a pigment → electrons are excited → the chain makes ATP and NADPH. Mark where water is split and where O₂ is released.
5
Record your walkthrough. Start a spatial recording. Walk through your chloroplast and explain aloud, in about 60–90 seconds: where each stage happens, and the light→electrons→ATP+NADPH path. Use the words thylakoid, stroma, and NADPH.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your spatial recording to this Canvas assignment. If you can’t record, submit one snapshot of your labeled chloroplast plus a few typed sentences naming where each stage happens and tracing the light→ATP+NADPH path.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 30 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students externalize chloroplast structure (double membrane, grana of thylakoids, stroma) and map the two stages to their locations, then trace the light-reaction energy path. Common sticking points: confusing thylakoid (light reactions) with stroma (Calvin cycle), and forgetting that the released O₂ comes from splitting water, not CO₂. Nudge students who place the Calvin cycle in the thylakoid. A strong submission shows correct structure, both stages correctly located, a traced light→ATP+NADPH path, and a recording that uses “thylakoid,” “stroma,” and “NADPH” correctly.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
A chloroplast is built with a double membrane, grana of stacked thylakoids, and the stroma around them.
☐
Both stages are correctly located (light reactions in the thylakoid, Calvin cycle in the stroma) and the light→ATP+NADPH path is traced.
☐
A spatial recording (or snapshot + written explanation) is submitted that explains the build.
