Week 8 · VR-B (Explore / Observe)
Explore a Leaf, Down to the Thylakoid
Solo VR activity · ~20 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔎 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will enter a leaf and travel inward, shrinking through four scales: the leaf surface → a mesophyll cell → a chloroplast → a single thylakoid. At each stop you’ll observe the structure and note where photosynthesis actually happens. Seeing the journey from a whole leaf down to the membrane where light is caught makes the chloroplast a real place, not just a diagram — and shows you the tissue where, in the leaf-disk assay, oxygen collects and floats a disk.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Reading 8.02 and Lesson 8.02 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Inside a Leaf — Scaling Down (Optima VR library)
On arrival: a Web URL Image IFX by the entry shows your explorer checklist and the four scale stops. Begin there.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Leaf surface. Stand on the leaf and look at its surface. Find the tiny pores (stomata) where CO₂ enters and O₂ and water vapor leave. Place a sticky note: what gas does the leaf take in for photosynthesis?
2
Mesophyll cell. Shrink and enter a mesophyll cell — the green inner leaf cell packed with chloroplasts. Note how many chloroplasts you can see. Place a sticky note: why are these cells where most photosynthesis happens?
3
Chloroplast. Enter one chloroplast. Identify its double membrane, the stacked grana, and the fluid stroma. Place a sticky note marking which region runs the Calvin cycle.
4
Thylakoid & capture your evidence. Travel onto a single thylakoid membrane — where light is caught and the light reactions run. Then take four named snapshots, one at each scale (leaf surface, mesophyll cell, chloroplast, thylakoid), naming each so you can use them later.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your four named snapshots to this Canvas assignment, plus a short typed note: name each scale in order, and in one sentence say where the light reactions happen versus where the Calvin cycle happens.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 20 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students travel down four scales (leaf → mesophyll cell → chloroplast → thylakoid) and observe where photosynthesis happens at each level. Common sticking points: confusing the stroma with the thylakoid, and taking a system screenshot rather than a named snapshot. Push students to connect what they see to function — stomata let CO₂ in, mesophyll cells are dense with chloroplasts, the thylakoid catches light. A complete submission shows four named snapshots and a note that correctly separates the light reactions (thylakoid) from the Calvin cycle (stroma). This activity gives students the structural picture behind the leaf-disk data in their Module 4 CER.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
All four scales are visited (leaf surface, mesophyll cell, chloroplast, thylakoid) and observed.
☐
A sticky note at each stop records the structure’s role in photosynthesis.
☐
Four named snapshots and the typed note (separating light reactions from Calvin cycle) are submitted to Canvas.
