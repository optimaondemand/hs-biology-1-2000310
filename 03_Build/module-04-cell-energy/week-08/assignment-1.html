The Cell’s Energy Currency

Before you begin: finish Reading 8.01 and work through Lesson 8.01, including the ATP⇔ADP cycle, anabolic vs. catabolic reactions, and how enzymes lower activation energy.
Lesson focus: ATP is the energy currency; enzymes are the tools that make a cell’s chemistry possible
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts: explain the ATP⇔ADP cycle in your own words, classify three reactions as anabolic or catabolic in a table, and write one sentence on how an enzyme lowers activation energy. The whole assignment should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
A cell cannot burn its food in one big blaze. So how does it store energy, spend it, and speed up the reactions that keep it alive?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — The ATP cycle in your own words (3 pts). In two to three sentences, explain the cycle ATP ⇔ ADP + phosphate + energy. Be sure to say which direction releases energy and which stores it, and where the energy is held (the bond to the third phosphate).
2
Part B — Classify the reactions (6 pts, 2 each). For each example, mark whether it is anabolic (builds / stores energy) or catabolic (breaks down / releases energy).
Reaction Anabolic or catabolic?
Breaking down a starch molecule into glucose units 
Linking amino acids together to build a protein 
A leaf building glucose from CO₂ and water 
3
Part C — Enzymes (1 pt). In one sentence, explain how an enzyme speeds a reaction. Use the phrase activation energy.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A correctly states that breaking the bond to the third phosphate releases energy (ATP→ADP) and reattaching it stores energy.
☐
Part B is correct: break-down = catabolic; build-up (protein, glucose) = anabolic.
☐
Part C says the enzyme lowers the activation energy of the reaction.
