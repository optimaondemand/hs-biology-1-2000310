📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment counts toward your laboratory work. In Part A you read the data table and answer two questions, citing specific numbers. In Part B you write a short mini-CER — a direct, smaller version of your Module 4 CER. The whole assignment should take about 20–25 minutes.
🧪 THE DATA
Ten leaf disks were sunk in each cup, then placed under different light levels. The table records how many disks (of 10) had floated back up at each 2-minute mark.

📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Read the data (4 pts, 2 each). Answer in full sentences, citing specific numbers from the table.
(a) Which light condition photosynthesized fastest? Give one number from the table that supports your answer.
(b) Why did the dark-control disks never float? Explain using what the light reactions need.
2
Part B — Mini-CER (6 pts). In three to five sentences, build a claim–evidence–reasoning argument answering: How does light intensity affect the rate of photosynthesis? Include a claim (e.g., more light → faster photosynthesis), evidence (specific numbers from the table, including the dark control), and reasoning (connect it to the mechanism: light powers the reactions that release the O₂ that floats the disks).
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A identifies high light as fastest and cites a real number (e.g., 10 floating by 8 min); explains the dark control by the absence of light.
☐
Part B states a clear claim and supports it with specific data, including the dark control.
☐
The reasoning ties faster floating to more oxygen made by the light-powered reactions.
