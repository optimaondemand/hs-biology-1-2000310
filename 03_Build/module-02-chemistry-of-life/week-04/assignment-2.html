Lipids & the Birth of the Membrane
Reading 4.01, Reading 4.02, and Lesson 4.02
Before you begin: finish Reading 4.02 and work through Lesson 4.02. You should know the three macromolecules that supply food energy — carbohydrates, fats (lipids), and proteins — and the difference between saturated and unsaturated fat.
Lesson focus: finding the four macromolecules in the real food on your shelf
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This is a short home investigation that counts toward your laboratory work. You will pick three real food products from your kitchen, read their nutrition labels, and identify which macromolecules they contain and which one dominates each food. Then you will use one label to explain the difference between saturated and unsaturated fat. The whole investigation should take about 25–30 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
The molecules you studied this week are not abstractions — they are the carbohydrates, fats, and proteins printed on every package in your kitchen. How can a nutrition label tell you which macromolecule dominates a food?
