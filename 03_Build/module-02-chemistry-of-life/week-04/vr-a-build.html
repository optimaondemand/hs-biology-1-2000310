Week 4 · VR-A (Build / Model)
Assemble the Four Macromolecules
Solo VR activity · ~30 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔬 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will build a short example of each of the four great macromolecules of life — a carbohydrate, a lipid, a protein, and a nucleic acid — from their monomer pieces. Reading about “monomers linking into polymers” is one thing; snapping the units together with your hands, and removing a water molecule to make the bond, makes dehydration synthesis something you can see. This is the same chemistry you met in Lesson 4.01, now built in space.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Lessons 4.01–4.04 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Molecular Modeling room (Optima VR library)
On arrival: go to the workbench marked by the Whiteboard IFX. A sticky note there gives your first instruction.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Build a carbohydrate. From the monomer IFX, take three or four glucose units and link them into a short chain to model starch. The single units are the monomers; the chain is the polymer.
2
Build a lipid and a protein. Assemble a triglyceride — one glycerol joined to three fatty acid tails. Then snap several amino acid IFX together with peptide bonds to make a short polypeptide.
3
Build a nucleic acid, then label everything. Link several nucleotide IFX into a short DNA segment. Now place a sticky note on each of your four builds naming the monomer and the polymer, and one sticky note marking a single dehydration-synthesis bond — the spot where a water molecule was removed to join two units.
4
Record your walkthrough. Start a spatial recording. Walk along your four builds and explain aloud, in about 60–90 seconds: the monomer and polymer of each macromolecule, and what dehydration synthesis does at the bond you marked.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your spatial recording to this Canvas assignment. If you can’t record, submit one snapshot of your four labeled builds plus a few typed sentences naming the monomer and polymer of each macromolecule and where dehydration synthesis happens.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 30 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: This activity asks students to externalize the monomer–polymer relationship as four spatial models, one per macromolecule. Common sticking points: snapping monomer IFX together, cloning a glucose or amino acid unit to make a chain, and starting a spatial recording. Watch for students who confuse the monomer with the polymer — nudge them to name the single repeating unit first, then the assembled chain. A strong submission shows all four builds (carbohydrate, lipid, protein, nucleic acid), each labeled with its monomer and polymer, and clearly marks one dehydration-synthesis bond.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
All four macromolecules are built: a carbohydrate chain, a triglyceride, a polypeptide, and a DNA segment.
☐
Each build is labeled with sticky notes naming its monomer and polymer, and one dehydration-synthesis bond is marked.
☐
A spatial recording (or snapshot + written explanation) is submitted that explains the builds.
