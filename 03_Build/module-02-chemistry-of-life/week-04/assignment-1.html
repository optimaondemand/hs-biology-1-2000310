Lesson focus: how monomers link into polymers, and how water builds and breaks those bonds
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts. In Part A you match each monomer to the polymer it builds. In Part B you decide whether each described reaction is dehydration synthesis or hydrolysis. In Part C you classify six carbohydrates as monosaccharide, disaccharide, or polysaccharide. The whole assignment should take about 20–25 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If a polymer is just a chain of repeating monomers, how does the cell decide when to build the chain and when to break it — and what does water have to do with both?
