📖 WHAT THIS BUILDS ON
Why Water Behaves
Reading 3.03 and Lesson 3.03
Before you begin: finish Reading 3.03 and work through Lesson 3.03, including its practice predicting how water responds to heat and to dissolved substances.
Lesson focus: how water’s polarity and hydrogen bonding explain its high specific heat, floating ice, and solvent power
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In Part A you complete a three-row chart: for each of water’s great properties — high specific heat, ice that floats, and the universal solvent — you name the property, identify its underlying cause in hydrogen bonding or polarity, and give one real-world or biological consequence. In Part B you write one short paragraph arguing which property matters most for life, and why. The whole assignment should take about 20–25 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Each of water’s strange behaviors traces back to the same bent, polar molecule — so which of them does the most to make life possible?
