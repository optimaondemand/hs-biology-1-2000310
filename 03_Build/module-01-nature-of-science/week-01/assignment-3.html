Assignment · 10 pts
📖 WHAT THIS BUILDS ON
From Hunch to Hypothesis
Reading 1.03 and Lesson 1.03
Before you begin: finish Reading 1.03 and Lesson 1.03, including the “How Testable?” activity.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
You will take a real biological question and build the thinking that turns it into an experiment: a sharp hypothesis, a clear prediction, the result that would prove you wrong, and a likely source of error. About 15 minutes.
🎯 YOUR QUESTION
Does the amount of light a bean plant receives each day affect how tall it grows?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Write a hypothesis (3 pts). Answer the question above with a testable hypothesis in “If…then…because” form. Your “because” should give a biological reason.
2
State a prediction (2 pts). Write the specific, measurable result you would expect to see if your hypothesis is correct. Make sure it is a prediction (an expected result), not a restatement of the hypothesis.
3
Name what would prove it wrong (3 pts). Describe the specific result that would falsify your hypothesis. This is the heart of the assignment: if you cannot name such a result, your hypothesis is not yet testable.
4
Identify one source of error (2 pts). Name one source of random or systematic error that could affect your measurements, label which type it is, and say how you would reduce it.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
The hypothesis is testable, uses “If…then…because,” and offers a biological reason.
☐
The prediction is specific and measurable, and is distinct from the hypothesis.
☐
A concrete falsifying result is named.
☐
One source of error is correctly labeled random or systematic, with a way to reduce it.
📊 GRADING RUBRIC
Criteria Proficient Developing Beginning Pts
Hypothesis & prediction
5 pts		 Testable hypothesis with reason; prediction specific, measurable, and distinct. Hypothesis or prediction present but vague, or the two overlap. Not testable, or only restates the question. 5 pts
Falsifiability
3 pts		 Names a concrete result that would prove the hypothesis wrong. Gestures at being wrong but the result is vague. No falsifying result given. 3 pts
Source of error
2 pts		 Error correctly labeled random/systematic with a way to reduce it. Error named but mislabeled or no reduction given. Missing. 2 pts
Total: 10 pts
