📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
You will take apart a short scientific argument into its Claim, Evidence, and Reasoning, judge how strong the reasoning is, and set up the scientific notebook you’ll keep all year. About 20 minutes. This is your last practice before the Module 1 CER writing assignment.
🎯 THE ARGUMENT TO ANALYZE
“Adding a small amount of sugar to vase water keeps cut carnations fresh longer. In a controlled test, ten carnations in sugar water stayed firm and colorful for an average of 9 days, while ten identical carnations in plain water from the same tap wilted after an average of 5 days. Everything else — the flowers, the room, the amount of water — was kept the same. The sugar most likely works because cut flowers can no longer make their own food through photosynthesis, so the dissolved sugar gives them an energy source their stems can still take up. (Some might say the sugar simply killed bacteria, but a separate group given only an antibacterial additive lasted just 6 days, so sugar’s effect was larger than the antibacterial effect alone.)”
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Label the CER (6 pts). Quote or paraphrase the argument’s claim, its evidence, and its reasoning — one labeled answer for each.
2
Evaluate the reasoning (3 pts). In two or three sentences, explain whether the reasoning successfully connects the evidence to the claim, and point out where the argument answers a counterargument. Is the argument convincing? Why or why not?
3
Set up your scientific notebook (3 pts). Create the notebook you’ll use all year (physical or a Word document in OneDrive). On the first page, write the course name, your name, today’s date, and one sentence stating the rule you will follow: record what actually happened, including surprises. Submit a photo or screenshot of that first page.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Claim, evidence, and reasoning are each correctly identified and clearly labeled.
☐
The evaluation explains the link between evidence and claim and points to where the counterargument is answered.
☐
A scientific notebook first page is created and submitted with the required header and honesty rule.
📊 GRADING RUBRIC
Criteria Proficient Developing Beginning Pts

Total: 12 pts
