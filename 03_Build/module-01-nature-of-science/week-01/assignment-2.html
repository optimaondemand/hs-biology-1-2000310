📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
You will read a flawed experiment, take it apart, and rebuild it. This is direct practice for the Module 1 writing assignment, where you will evaluate a flawed design in full. Expect about 20 minutes.
🎯 THE EXPERIMENT TO EVALUATE
“Maria wants to know whether listening to music helps plants grow. She puts one plant next to a speaker playing music for eight hours a day on her warm, sunny kitchen counter, and keeps a second plant with no music in a cool hallway closet. She waters the kitchen plant daily and the closet plant twice a week. After three weeks, the kitchen plant is 9 cm taller. Maria concludes that music makes plants grow taller.”
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Name the variables (4 pts). State Maria’s intended independent variable and her dependent variable, then list at least two variables that should have been controlled but were not.
2
Diagnose the flaw (4 pts). In two or three sentences, explain why Maria’s conclusion is not trustworthy. Use the word confounded and name at least one specific factor — other than music — that could explain why the kitchen plant grew taller.
3
Redesign it as a fair test (4 pts). Rewrite Maria’s experiment so it actually tests her question. Your design must change only the independent variable, name what is held constant, include a control group, and say what she should measure. Mention one way she could make the result more trustworthy (e.g., more plants, repeating the test).
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Independent and dependent variables are correctly identified, with at least two uncontrolled variables named.
☐
The diagnosis uses “confounded” correctly and names a specific alternative cause.
☐
The redesign changes only one variable, holds the rest constant, includes a control group, and names the measurement.
📊 GRADING RUBRIC
Criteria Proficient Developing Beginning Pts
Variables
4 pts		 IV and DV correct; two or more uncontrolled variables named. IV and DV mostly correct; one uncontrolled variable named. IV/DV confused or missing. 4 pts
Diagnosis
4 pts		 Clearly explains the confounding and names a specific alternative cause. Identifies a problem but explanation is vague or incomplete. No clear reason the conclusion fails. 4 pts
Redesign
4 pts		 Changes one variable, holds rest constant, includes control group and measurement. Improved but missing the control group or a constant. Still changes more than one variable. 4 pts
Total: 12 pts
