Week 23 · VR-B (Explore / Observe)
Tour a Plant from Root to Leaf
Solo VR activity · ~20 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔎 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will walk a whole plant, from the deepest root to the highest leaf, and trace the three tissue systems — dermal, ground, and vascular — as they run continuously through it. In the root you'll find the dermal epidermis (with root hairs), ground storage tissue, and the vascular core. In the stem you'll find the vascular bundles. In the leaf you'll find ground tissue (mesophyll) and the veins. The one big idea to carry away: the same three systems thread through the entire plant, like an organ system running through a body. This is Lessons 23.01–23.03, observed in space.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Lessons 23.01–23.03 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Whole Plant Walk (Optima VR library)
On arrival: a Web URL Image IFX by the entry shows your explorer checklist and the three tissue systems to look for. Begin underground at the Root marker.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Root. Walk into the root and find all three systems: the dermal epidermis with its root hairs (absorption), the ground storage tissue filling the bulk, and the vascular core running up the center. Take a snapshot named “Root.” Note which tissue systems you see.
2
Stem. Climb up the stem and find the vascular bundles carrying materials between root and leaf, wrapped in dermal covering and ground tissue. Take a snapshot named “Stem.” Note which tissue systems you see — and that they are the same ones from the root, unbroken.
3
Leaf. Follow a vascular bundle out into a leaf. Find the ground tissue (the chloroplast-rich mesophyll) and the veins (vascular), all under the dermal epidermis. Take a snapshot named “Leaf.” Note which tissue systems you see.
4
The big idea. At the summary marker, answer in a sentence: which tissue system did you find in all three parts — root, stem, and leaf? (Hint: you should be able to trace the vascular tissue — and in fact all three systems — the whole way.)
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your three named snapshots (Root, Stem, Leaf) to this Canvas assignment, plus a short typed note: for each snapshot, list which tissue systems appear, and write one sentence naming the tissue system(s) you could trace continuously through all three parts.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 20 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: The whole point of this walk is the module's big idea — that the same three tissue systems run through the entire plant, from root to stem to leaf, rather than each part having its own separate anatomy. Encourage students to physically trace the vascular tissue from the root core, up the stem bundles, and out into the leaf veins so they feel its continuity. A complete submission shows three correctly named snapshots (Root, Stem, Leaf), each with the tissue systems identified, and a note that correctly recognizes the systems (especially vascular) running unbroken through all three.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
All three parts are visited in order (root, stem, leaf) and the three tissue systems are looked for in each.
☐
Three named snapshots (Root, Stem, Leaf) are captured, each noting which tissue systems appear.
☐
The typed note correctly identifies the tissue system(s) that run continuously through all three parts of the plant.
