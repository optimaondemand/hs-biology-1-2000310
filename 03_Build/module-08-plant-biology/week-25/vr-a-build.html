Week 25 · VR-A (Build / Model)
Build a Flower; Show Double Fertilization & Seed
Solo VR activity · ~30 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔬 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will build a flower in 3D and label every part, then animate the story of reproduction from pollination all the way to seed and fruit. First you assemble and name the parts — sepal, petal, stamen (anther + filament), and carpel/pistil (stigma + style + ovary + ovules). Then you show the sequence: pollen lands on the stigma, a pollen tube grows down, double fertilization occurs, and the ovule becomes a seed while the ovary becomes a fruit. Building it in space makes the whole reproductive path concrete. This is Lessons 25.01 & 25.02, built in space.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Lessons 25.01 & 25.02 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Flower & Seed Lab (Optima VR library)
On arrival: go to the empty flower-build bench marked by the Whiteboard IFX. A sticky note there gives your first instruction.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Build the outer parts. Place the sepals (label: protect the bud) and the petals (label: attract pollinators) around the outside of your flower.
2
Build the male part. Add the stamen: the anther (label: male — makes pollen) on its filament stalk. Mark the whole stamen M.
3
Build the female part. Add the carpel/pistil at the center: stigma (label: sticky — catches pollen), style, and ovary with ovules inside (label: holds the eggs). Mark the whole carpel F.
4
Animate the sequence. Using the lab’s animation tools, show, in order: pollen lands on the stigma → a pollen tube grows down the style → double fertilization (one sperm → zygote/embryo; the other sperm → endosperm) → the ovule becomes a seed and the ovary becomes a fruit.
5
Record your walkthrough. Start a spatial recording. In about 60–90 seconds, name each flower part and its role, then narrate the sequence — being sure to name the two products of double fertilization (embryo and endosperm) and what the ovule and ovary each become.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your spatial recording to this Canvas assignment. If you can’t record, submit one snapshot of your fully labeled flower plus a few typed sentences naming each part, the two products of double fertilization, and what the ovule and ovary become.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 30 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students externalize flower structure and the reproductive sequence by building and narrating them. Watch for two common errors. First, the male/female mix-up: the stamen (anther + filament) is male; the carpel/pistil (stigma + style + ovary) is female — correct any reversed labels. Second, the pollination-vs-fertilization confusion: pollination is only pollen moving from anther to stigma; fertilization is the sperm joining the egg (and, in flowering plants, the second sperm forming endosperm). A strong submission shows a correctly labeled flower and a walkthrough that names the two fertilization products (embryo, endosperm) and states ovule → seed, ovary → fruit.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
The flower is built with all parts placed and labeled, with the stamen marked male and the carpel/pistil marked female.
☐
The animated sequence runs in order: pollen on stigma → pollen tube → double fertilization → seed and fruit.
☐
A spatial recording (or snapshot + written explanation) is submitted that names the parts and the two fertilization products (embryo and endosperm).
