Flower Structure and Pollination
Reading 25.01 and Lesson 25.01
Before you begin: finish Reading 25.01 and work through Lesson 25.01, including the parts of a flower, their roles, and which parts are male and which are female.
Lesson focus: A rooted plant’s answer to reproduction
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has two short parts: match each flower part to its role, and mark which parts are male and which are female. It should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What is each part of a flower for — and which parts are the male and female reproductive structures?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Part to role (6 pts, 1 each). Match each flower part to its correct role from the list below.
Flower part Role (write the letter)

2
Part B — Male or female (4 pts, 1 each). For each structure below, write M (male) or F (female): stamen, anther, carpel/pistil, stigma.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A: sepal → A, petal → B, anther → C, filament → D, stigma → E, ovary → F.
☐
Part B: stamen → M, anther → M, carpel/pistil → F, stigma → F. (Stamen = anther + filament = male; carpel/pistil = stigma + style + ovary = female.)
