Lesson focus: Two sperm, two products — embryo and endosperm
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts: put the reproductive events in order, name the two products of double fertilization, and match each flower part to what it becomes. It should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
How does a grain of pollen on a stigma become a seed inside a fruit — and what are the two products of double fertilization?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Put the events in order (5 pts). Number these five events 1–5 in the order they happen:
• The ovule becomes a seed and the ovary becomes a fruit
• Pollen is transferred from an anther to a stigma (pollination)
• Double fertilization occurs (embryo and endosperm form)
• A pollen tube grows down the style toward an ovule
• Two sperm cells are delivered to the ovule
2
Part B — Name the two products (2 pts). In double fertilization, the first sperm makes one product and the second sperm makes another. Name each product and, in a few words, say what each becomes or does.
3
Part C — What becomes what (3 pts, 1 each). Match each flower part to what it develops into after fertilization.

✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A order: (1) pollination, (2) pollen tube grows down the style, (3) two sperm delivered to the ovule, (4) double fertilization, (5) ovule → seed and ovary → fruit.
☐
Part B: first sperm + egg → zygote/embryo (the future plant); second sperm + two polar nuclei → endosperm (food that nourishes the embryo).
☐
Part C: ovule → B (seed), ovary → A (fruit), zygote → C (embryo).
