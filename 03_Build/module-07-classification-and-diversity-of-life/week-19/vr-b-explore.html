Week 19 · VR-B (Explore / Observe)
Explore the Specimen Room: Key Out Five Unknowns
Solo VR activity · ~20 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔎 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will enter a specimen room holding five unknown organisms on labeled stands (marked A–E). Using a dichotomous key provided at the entrance, you will work each specimen through the key’s paired, observable choices until you reach its name — then capture a named snapshot of each identification. This is Lesson 19.03’s skill put to work: observe carefully, choose the statement that fits, and follow the key to a single answer. No guessing — only observation.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Reading 19.03 and Lesson 19.03 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Specimen Room — Identification Lab (Optima VR library)
On arrival: a Web URL Image IFX by the entry displays the dichotomous key and your explorer checklist. Begin at specimen stand A.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Read the key. At the entrance, study the posted dichotomous key. Note that every step is an either/or choice about something you can see (leaf shape, number of legs, wings, shell, and so on).
2
Work Specimen A through the key. Observe it closely, start at step 1, choose the statement that fits, and follow where it sends you — step by step — until the key gives a name.
3
Capture a named snapshot. Take a snapshot of Specimen A and name the file with your identification and the steps you followed (for example, “A_gardenspider_1b-3a”).
4
Repeat for Specimens B, C, D, and E. Key out each one, and capture a named snapshot for each — five identifications in all.
5
Note the traits. In your notebook, jot the one observable trait that made the final decision for each specimen — this is your evidence that the key, not a guess, gave the answer.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your five named snapshots (one per specimen, A–E) to this Canvas assignment, plus a short typed note listing each specimen’s identification and the one observable trait that clinched it.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 20 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students apply a dichotomous key to five unknown specimens, practicing careful observation and step-by-step decision-making rather than recognition. The key strength to reinforce is repeatability: because every step rests on an observable, either/or trait, any student should reach the same five names. Common sticking points: skipping ahead instead of following the numbered path, and answering from prior guesses rather than from what is visible. A complete submission shows five correctly named snapshots (A–E) and a note naming the deciding observable trait for each. This walk sets up next week’s move to the kingdoms and domains themselves.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
All five specimens (A–E) are worked through the key and identified.
☐
Five named snapshots are captured, one per specimen, labeled with the identification.
☐
The typed note lists each identification and the deciding observable trait, and is submitted to Canvas with the snapshots.
