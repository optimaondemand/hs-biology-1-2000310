Week 21 · VR-B (Explore / Observe)
Explore Habitats of the Microbial World
Solo VR activity · ~20 minutes · 10 pts (completion)
🔎 WHAT YOU’RE DOING
You will visit four habitat scenes and meet the organisms of this week's four groups where they actually live: a hot spring (Archaea, the extremophiles), a pond (Bacteria and animal-like and plant-like protists such as Amoeba, Paramecium, and drifting algae), and forest soil (decomposer Bacteria and Fungi — mushrooms and the mycelium). At each, you will observe representative organisms and capture named snapshots. Seeing them in place makes the diversity of the microbial and fungal world real. This is Lessons 21.02–21.04, observed in space.
🔧 WHAT YOU’LL NEED
A VR headset (recommended) or the ENGAGE desktop app · your completed Readings 21.02–21.04 · your scientific notebook.
📍 HOW TO JOIN
Device: VR headset or desktop · Session type: temporary
Session ID: posted by your teacher at the top of this Canvas assignment
Location: Habitats of Life — Microbial World Walk (Optima VR library)
On arrival: a Web URL Image IFX by the entry shows your explorer checklist and the four habitat markers. Begin at the hot spring.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Station 1 — Hot Spring (Archaea). Observe the steaming, near-boiling water and the colorful microbial mats at its edge. Place a sticky note: which domain's extremophiles thrive here, and why is this habitat “extreme”?
2
Station 2 — Pond (Bacteria & animal-like protists). Look for Amoeba engulfing prey and Paramecium swimming. Place a sticky note naming one animal-like protist and how it feeds or moves.
3
Station 3 — Sunlit Water (plant-like protists). Observe drifting phytoplankton (algae) in the sunlit surface layer. Place a sticky note: why are these tiny producers so important to the whole ocean?
4
Station 4 — Forest Soil (Fungi) & capture your evidence. Observe mushrooms and the threadlike mycelium spreading through the leaf litter, breaking down dead wood. Then take four named snapshots, one per station, naming each by its habitat and the group it shows.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
Upload your four named snapshots to this Canvas assignment, plus a short typed note: list the four habitats and, for each, name the group you observed (Archaea, Bacteria/protists, phytoplankton, Fungi) and one thing it does.
⏱️ TIME · ABOUT 20 MINUTES IN-HEADSET
Facilitator note: Students observe representatives of the week's four groups in their habitats, connecting each organism to its ecological role. The most useful corrections: extremophiles are not “super-bacteria” (many are Archaea, a separate domain), and phytoplankton are producers, not tiny animals. A complete submission shows four correctly named snapshots and a note that pairs each habitat with the right group and a role. This walk gives students a firsthand look at the diversity they described in Lessons 21.02–21.04.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA (COMPLETION)
☐
All four habitats are visited (hot spring, pond, sunlit water, forest soil) and observed.
☐
A sticky note at each station records an observation linking the organism to its group or role.
☐
Four correctly named snapshots and the typed note (habitat + group + role) are submitted to Canvas.
