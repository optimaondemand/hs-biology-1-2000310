📖 WHAT THIS BUILDS ON
Florida Biodiversity Across Kingdoms
Reading 22.04 and Lesson 22.04
Before you begin: finish Reading 22.04 and work through Lesson 22.04, including Florida organisms across the domains and kingdoms and how AI species-identification tools help and fail.
Lesson focus: The whole tree of life, gathered in one state
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three short parts: match Florida organisms to their kingdom or domain, add one example of your own, and explain one limitation of AI species-identification tools. It should take about 15–20 minutes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
How does one Florida landscape hold nearly the whole tree of life — and how far can we trust an app to name it?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Part A — Match organism to group (5 pts, 1 each). Match each Florida organism to its kingdom or domain from the list below.
Florida organism Kingdom / domain (write the letter)
American alligator 
Mangrove tree 
Mushroom on a rotting log 
Phytoplankton in an estuary 
Nucleus-free single cells in a spring 
A. Animals  ·  B. Plants  ·  C. Fungi  ·  D. Protists  ·  E. Bacteria / Archaea (domains)
2
Part B — Add your own (2 pts). Name one more real Florida organism (not already listed) and state its kingdom or domain.
3
Part C — Interrogate the AI (3 pts). In one or two sentences, describe one way an AI species-identification app (such as iNaturalist) can fail or mislead, and state what a careful scientist should do before trusting its answer.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Part A: alligator → A, mangrove → B, mushroom → C, phytoplankton → D, nucleus-free single cells → E.
☐
Part B names a real Florida organism with the correct kingdom/domain (e.g., gopher tortoise → Animals; saw palmetto or seagrass → Plants; roseate spoonbill → Animals).
☐
